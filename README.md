# Customer Success Platform

A single system for running customers through the pre-sales → post-sales lifecycle,
and for giving leadership a truthful view of every account without asking anyone.

Two goals drive every design decision:

- **Nothing falls through the cracks** — commitments made during the sale are typed
  records that survive the handoff, and the system escalates on its own.
- **Leadership sees reality** — account health is derived from objective signals,
  never self-reported.
